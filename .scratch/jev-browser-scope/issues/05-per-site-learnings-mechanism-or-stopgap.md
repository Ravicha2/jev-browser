# Are per-site learnings the mechanism, or a stopgap?

**Type:** grilling
**Status:** open
**Blocked by:** none

## Question

`SKILL.md:102-107` makes `learnings/<site>/` the place a fix goes after a round on that site escalates. Is that the intended mechanism for the gated-collection mode, or is it evidence that the loop is too brittle to carry a scope claim?

Why it matters to the destination: if every new site needs a hand-written rule before it converges, the statement cannot claim anything general about collection, and the honest claim is much narrower than the current wording. If the rules do converge and amortize, the claim survives, and the convention then needs a written bar for what a rule must contain before it is trusted.

## What a resolution looks like

A decision on one of:

1. Per-site learnings are the mechanism. The statement says so, and specifies the required shape of a rule.
2. They are a stopgap. The statement names what replaces them.
3. They are out of scope for this effort, and the question moves to the fog.

## Note

`learnings/` currently holds only `README.md`, so there is no evidence in hand either way. If the decision needs evidence, that is a fresh round on a gated site, and the run belongs in 06.
